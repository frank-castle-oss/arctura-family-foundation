# Ground-Operations Pivot

> **Formation-stage notice.** Arctura Family Foundation is a working name only. No legal entity, live food-delivery service, payment operation, automated outreach program, or public impact claim is represented by this document.

## Purpose

Arctura’s near-term work is to strengthen the local conditions that make food-access coordination responsible: accountable governance, informed partners, food-handling readiness, verified referrals, and human stewardship. Commercial tooling is optional. It is not a prerequisite for this work.

| Operating layer | First proof | Activation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance | A named human owner and recorded formation/fiscal-path decision | No representation of legal formation until complete. |
| Community readiness | Local listening across leadership, existing efforts, community knowledge, climate, resources, and issue knowledge | No city is called “ready” from desk research alone. [1] |
| Food-recovery partnerships | Verified donor, recovery organization, and carrier/service relationships; agreement and recordkeeping readiness | No pickup or delivery claim before written, local controls exist. [2] [3] |
| Referral navigation | A current directory of existing resources and a consent-aware manual handoff protocol | No sensitive data-sharing or closed-loop referral system without partner consent and governance. [4] [5] |
| Evidence | Traceable records for quantities, handoffs, exceptions, and verification | No impact publication without evidence. |

## City Starting Points

| City | Existing capacity observed | Appropriate starting role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Los Angeles | The City’s edible-surplus-food framework describes partner, agreement, donation-record, and recovery-record expectations. [3] | Form one accountable local partner chain before considering a pilot. |
| Austin | The City publishes donation-handling guidance; local food-recovery organizations already connect surplus food and neighbors. [6] [7] | Map unmet donor/recipient gaps and food-handling readiness before adding a coordination layer. |
| Denver | Denver directs residents to existing 211, pantry, meal, benefit, and delivery resources. [8] | Prioritize verified referral navigation, not a competing delivery promise. |

## Technology Principle

Arctura should use the **smallest viable operating path**. Existing local resources and a protected internal evidence log come before a new platform. A shared referral system or food-recovery product should be considered only with participating partners, a data owner, consent rules, an adoption plan, and an exit path. Research on referral platforms emphasizes partner engagement, workflow fit, privacy procedures, and the time required for adoption. [4] [5]

| Tool category | Possible future role | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open-source nonprofit CRM | Relationship, grants, and constituent stewardship | Documented data owner, hosting/security capacity, and lifecycle policy. |
| Food-recovery coordination product | Donor-to-pickup recordkeeping and coordination | Local pilot, food-safety review, written partner commitments, and named operational owner. |
| Commercial catalog | Clearly non-charitable educational or service offering | Legal structure, separated finance controls, explicitly approved products, and no connection to recipient eligibility. |
