# Arctura Family Foundation — Staged Operating Stack

**Working recommendation.** “Arctura Family Foundation” is a working name only; the legal entity is pending creation and final-name selection. This is a build and diligence sequence, not a procurement award or commitment to spend.

## Executive Decision

Arctura should establish a verified U.S. pilot cell before global expansion. The Foundation ledger and the operating platform remain distinct. Enterprise systems enter only when measured program complexity requires them.

| Operating Domain | Pilot Recommendation | Expansion Candidate | Activation Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legal fundraising | Fiscal sponsor or recognized charitable entity | Country-specific charitable partners | Signed sponsor agreement or confirmed tax-exempt status |
| Donor support | Nonprofit contribution platform | Global project-funding platform for qualified projects | Legal eligibility and fund-restriction map |
| Commercial transactions | Shopify catalog and checkout | Local merchant arrangements by country | Claim store, merchant verification, public policies |
| Partner and donor CRM | HubSpot-style CRM | Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud | Data model, consent policy, owner, approved workflows |
| Foundation finance | Nonprofit fund-accounting system | Multi-entity fund-accounting / ERP | Chart of accounts, approval matrix, accountant selection |
| Food recovery | City-level nonprofit / food-recovery partner | National and international partner network | Food-safety SOP, partner agreement, traceability rules |
| Last-mile delivery | Controlled city carrier pilot | Multi-carrier routing by country | Verified pick-up sites, fulfillment SOP, insurance |
| Customer service | Founder-led service desk and partner liaison | Managed multilingual provider after validated volume | Knowledge base, escalation map, privacy terms, QA scorecard |
| Demand generation | Mission content, local pages, referral partners | Verified nonprofit marketing programs | Eligible status, analytics, approved public claims |

## Finance Design

| Cashflow Type | System of Record | Recipient-Facing Language |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charitable gift | Eligible nonprofit or fiscal-sponsor contribution system | “Support food access” only when eligibility is verified |
| Grant | Fund-accounting system | Not customer-facing |
| Corporate sponsorship | Contract plus accounting records | “Sponsor a program” only when terms are clear |
| Paid good or service | Shopify plus accounting system | “Purchase” or “service” |
| Vendor or carrier payment | Accounting/AP system | Not customer-facing |
| Recipient food access | Program and fulfillment records | “No-cost food access” only in activated service areas |

## Procurement Sequence

1. Confirm entity or fiscal sponsor, governance lead, accountant, and city-pilot criteria.
2. Select one food-recovery partner and one recipient-distribution partner in the pilot city.
3. Select one support channel, one CRM, and one finance system—not competing systems.
4. Run a controlled delivery pilot with one carrier before a technical carrier integration.
5. Activate broad marketing only after capacity, consent, and response ownership are real.
