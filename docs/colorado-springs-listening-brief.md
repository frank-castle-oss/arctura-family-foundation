# Colorado Springs Local Listening Research

**Scope.** This is a formation-stage readiness brief for Colorado Springs, Colorado. It does not announce a pilot, delivery service, grant program, payment option, public partnership, or legal entity.

## Verified Existing Public Route

El Paso County Department of Human Services identifies SNAP, Colorado Works, Health First Colorado, and other county-administered programs; it directs applications through Colorado PEAK, in-person channels, or mail. This means Arctura should treat public-benefit navigation as an existing system to understand and reference—not as an eligibility function to duplicate. [1]

| Existing route | Proper formation-stage posture for Arctura |
| --- | --- |
| El Paso County DHS / Colorado PEAK | Refer only to the official pathway; do not assess eligibility, collect benefit documents, or represent county services. |
| Public food-support navigation | Validate current availability and accessibility with local informants before linking, listing, or routing a person. |
| Potential food-recovery or distribution collaborators | Research role, safety procedures, capacity, consent, and willingness before calling any organization a partner. |

Pikes Peak United Way describes 211 as a free, confidential, multilingual navigation service for El Paso County and other counties. It states that trained information specialists use a resource database that includes government, nonprofit, faith, and community-based providers. Its public page also describes existing food-distribution activity with Care and Share Food Bank. Arctura should therefore make 211 and existing local navigators the first inquiry point for an urgent food-access request, not a parallel intake that claims to replace them. [2]

Food to Power publicly describes work across food access, food education, and food production, including redistribution of surplus food to neighborhood food-access programs created with residents. This establishes a local organization to understand through a consent-based listening conversation; it is **not** evidence of an Arctura partnership, available capacity, or an authorization to use Food to Power’s services in a proposed workflow. [3]

| Existing system or organization | Publicly described role | Formation-stage implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pikes Peak United Way / 211 | Confidential, multilingual resource navigation across El Paso County; current database of local service providers | Treat as a primary existing referral route and ask about referral boundaries before any proposed handoff. |
| Care and Share Food Bank | Existing regional food-distribution collaborator cited by Pikes Peak United Way | Establish its current service model and partner protocols before considering any overlap. |
| Food to Power | Food access, food recovery, education, food production, and no-cost grocery activity | Learn from its resident-centered model and ask whether an introduction would be useful; do not assume a role or capacity. |

Care and Share describes a regional system that gathers and sorts food before distributing through hundreds of partner agencies across Southern Colorado. It also directs the public to partner pantries, meal sites, and mobile markets. Arctura should learn how its agency and referral boundaries work before it describes any coverage, donor pathway, or distribution model in Colorado Springs. [4]

El Paso County Public Health states that its environmental-health specialists inspect retail food facilities, educate food handlers, investigate complaints, and assist with outbreak investigations. Any future food-recovery or prepared-food concept must therefore be preceded by a food-safety listening conversation and a documented handling question; it must not be built from a web-only assumption. [5]

Silver Key’s Meals on Wheels page describes an existing home-delivered-meal program and resource-navigation route for older adults in the Pikes Peak region. It is an important perspective for mobility, scheduling, wellness-check, and affordability questions, but it is not an invitation for Arctura to approach its recipients, delivery network, or payment model. [6]

| Existing system or organization | Publicly described role | Formation-stage implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care and Share Food Bank | Southern Colorado food bank serving a partner-agency network, pantries, meal sites, and mobile markets | Learn existing distribution and agency-boundary practices before suggesting a parallel recovery or donor model. |
| El Paso County Public Health | Retail-food inspection, handler education, complaint investigation, and foodborne-outbreak support | Place food safety before recovery, delivery, or public service-design claims. |
| Silver Key Senior Services | Home-delivered meals and resource navigation for older adults | Include a mobility and older-adult perspective; do not duplicate its screening, delivery, or payment flow. |

## Initial Listening Question

What is already working in Colorado Springs, where do people encounter avoidable navigation or access barriers, and which accountable local people would need to agree on a safe, minimum-data pathway before Arctura proposes any new operating layer?

## Proposed Listening Map

The following are **roles to invite only after the user explicitly approves a manual outreach plan**. They are not named contacts, prospective partners, endorsements, or commitments. The target is six to eight conversations, each with a defined question and a no-data-sharing default.

| Listening category | Accountable role to seek | Question Arctura needs answered | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public-benefits navigation | El Paso County DHS or Colorado PEAK navigation representative | Where does food-benefit navigation break down, and what can a community navigator accurately say without assessing eligibility? | Do not collect applications, documents, or eligibility information. |
| Community resource referral | Pikes Peak United Way 211 information-and-referral lead | When is 211 the correct handoff, what details may be shared with consent, and what resource changes matter most? | Do not duplicate the directory or imply integration. |
| Regional distribution | Care and Share partner-agency or community-program liaison | Which partner capacities, intake rules, safety requirements, and geography constraints must a local coordinator understand? | Do not imply agency affiliation or request recipient lists. |
| Community-led food access and recovery | Food to Power program or community-engagement leader | What does resident co-creation require, and what unfilled coordination questions should an outsider avoid solving alone? | Do not use the organization’s name, services, or contact base as an Arctura asset. |
| Food safety and environmental health | El Paso County Public Health retail-food or environmental-health representative | What handling, inspection, licensing, recall, and escalation questions apply before any recovery or delivery model is discussed? | Do not characterize a future model as compliant or approved. |
| Homebound and older-adult access | Silver Key meals or resource-navigation leader | What mobility, scheduling, check-in, affordability, and emergency-continuity factors distinguish home-delivered meals from a generic delivery concept? | Do not refer people, quote costs, or propose a payment flow. |
| Neighborhood trust and lived expertise | Resident leader or trusted neighborhood-based organization selected with local guidance | Which access barriers, language needs, schedules, and trust conditions are invisible in service directories? | Compensate and obtain consent before using any lived experience in planning or publication. |
| Frontline pantry or meal-site operations | A current local pantry, meal-site, shelter, or community-center operating lead selected through approved channels | What happens at the last mile: hours, transportation, documentation, safety, exception handling, and referral failures? | Do not solicit recipient data or promise supplemental capacity. |

## Next Gate

Colorado Springs is the **proposed first listening geography**, not a live city. Before anyone is contacted, Arctura needs a named internal outreach owner, a short consent statement, an interview guide, a note-retention rule, and approval to send manual invitations. After six to eight conversations, the next output should be a private evidence summary with three options: continue mapping, formalize a limited referral-navigation partnership discussion, or pause. No option authorizes food recovery, delivery, payments, fundraising, or public launch without separate governance, safety, privacy, and capacity evidence.
