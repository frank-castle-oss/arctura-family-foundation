# Arctura Family Foundation — Visual Rights and Attribution Standard

> **Name status:** “Arctura Family Foundation” is a working name only. The legal entity is pending creation and final-name selection.

## Purpose

Arctura may use supplied imagery, audio, and creative files only in a way that respects identity, consent, provenance, and the public’s right not to be misled. A file being available to the project is **not** sufficient evidence that it is cleared for public publication.

## Publication rule

No supplied leadership photograph, campaign image, recording, signature, identity-bearing visual, or account-export file may be published until the following record exists:

| Required field | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Asset identifier | Stable file name or internal asset record |
| Rights holder | Named person or entity with authority to grant use |
| Consent scope | Website, public repository, social media, press, paid media, or internal only |
| Attribution | Exact display name, role, and any required credit line |
| Context | The approved statement the asset may support; no implied endorsement beyond it |
| Territory and duration | Geographic and time limits, if any |
| Withdrawal path | Contact and process for revoking future use where legally and operationally possible |

## Naming and role rules

Arctura must never infer a person’s identity, title, employment, relationship to the foundation, credentials, location, or endorsement from an image, filename, account export, or visual resemblance. Public captions must use only the approved attribution record. If the record is incomplete, the asset remains private.

## Sensitive assets

Account exports, credential-adjacent files, recordings containing personal voice, and images that could identify a recipient, employee, volunteer, or partner are restricted by default. They are excluded from public repositories, product listings, automated model inputs, and public site deployment unless separately cleared.

## Editorial integrity

Images may support approved facts or clearly labeled concepts. They may not be used to imply that Arctura has delivered services, partnered with an organization, received funding, served a community, or obtained an endorsement when that claim is not verified and documented.

## Implementation status

The supplied assets are currently treated as **private and unapproved for public use**. The public Arctura site uses no supplied leadership or campaign imagery until the asset records above are completed.
