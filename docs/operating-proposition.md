# Arctura Operating Proposition

**Formation-stage boundary.** Arctura Family Foundation is a working name only. This proposition does not claim that Arctura is a formed entity, food provider, delivery service, payment processor, grantmaker, or active emergency-response program.

## Mission

> **Arctura builds accountable local infrastructure so neighbors can navigate existing food support and communities can prepare safe, dignified food-recovery partnerships before anyone is asked to depend on a new service.**

This mission treats food access as a coordinated local system rather than a marketplace. It aligns with federal Community Food Projects guidance that calls for food-insecure community members to participate in planning, design, implementation, and evaluation, and with public food-access programs that emphasize coordination, barrier identification, and local resource navigation. [1] [2]

## Portfolio

The present portfolio is **not a product catalog**. It is a formation-stage set of controlled operating capabilities.

| Portfolio line | Intended participant | Present status | What is delivered now | What it does not do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local readiness mapping | Community organizations and civic collaborators | Formation | Evidence gates, local-system research, gap mapping, and accountable next steps | Certify a city as live or make a service promise |
| Partner qualification | Food donors, recipient organizations, carriers, and specialist providers | Available for manual review | Structured interest intake and human review of capacity, boundaries, and evidence | Create a partner agreement or dispatch food |
| Referral navigation | Neighbors seeking food-access information | Limited, manual | Minimal-data request intake and referral routing toward verified existing local resources | Determine eligibility, provide emergency response, or promise delivery |
| Stewardship and coordination | Funders, sponsors, service collaborators, and press | Available for manual review | Human follow-up, separated finance context, and documented next steps | Accept a payment, promise tax treatment, or automate solicitation |
| Evidence and learning | Partners and public-interest observers | Formation | Controlled operating records, public research, and verified narrative standards | Publish impact without substantiation |
| Commercial services | Institutional clients | Inactive | None; future advisory, reporting, training, or coordination services may be considered only after approval | Fund recipient access through a storefront or condition help on purchase |

## Who Serves Whom

Arctura must not invent a staff roster. Today, the **provisional human route is stewardship@arctura.org**. The operating model assigns roles, not names, until accountable people are formally appointed.

| Participant group | Human service owner | First response | Required escalation or handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neighbor seeking food support | Stewardship reviewer; later a city lead | Acknowledge the request and identify verified local navigation options | Existing 211, public pantry locator, local provider, or emergency service as appropriate; no delivery commitment |
| Food donor or recovery partner | Partner-qualification owner | Review donor type, handling needs, geography, capacity, and documentation | Food-safety and logistics owner before any operational commitment |
| Recipient organization or carrier | Partner-qualification owner | Confirm role, capacity, and safe handoff boundaries | City lead and incident/escalation owner before any coordination activity |
| Funder, sponsor, or grantmaker | Stewardship and finance-control owner | Clarify the inquiry type and legal/fiscal boundary | Governance lead before any gift, grant, sponsorship, or commercial commitment |
| Service collaborator or press | Stewardship reviewer | Provide the working-name, launch-state, and evidence boundary | Appropriate accountable lead once appointed |

## How People Find Arctura

The discovery strategy is **trust-first and non-automated**. The first goal is to be findable by the right local people, not to create volume that the forming organization cannot responsibly serve.

| Path | Audience | Asset | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clear public site and city pages | Partners and local collaborators | Mission, readiness boundaries, partner-intake form, and city research pages | Do not represent a city as active before local gates are met |
| Public documentation and Field Notes | Funders, journalists, systems collaborators | Source-aware operating research, governance standards, and public GitHub documentation | No fabricated testimonials, performance claims, or donor promises |
| Existing local resource directories | Neighbors and navigators | Verified links to 211, public food-assistance maps, and established providers | Add or claim a listing only when the service description, owner, and response capacity are real |
| Partner introductions and listening | Donors, recipient organizations, carriers, community leaders | One-to-one, consent-aware conversations guided by a local readiness brief | No automated cold outreach; do not harvest contacts or infer consent |
| Institutional opportunities | Grantmakers and civic collaborators | Formation-stage concept note, readiness evidence, and separated finance model | Do not apply, solicit, or claim eligibility until the legal/fiscal pathway allows it |

Public food-access programs show the value of understandable, current resource navigation; for example, Denver points residents to 211, pantry locators, benefits, and specific existing food-delivery resources rather than creating a generic claim of universal coverage. [3] Referral-system research further cautions that implementation depends on partner participation, privacy procedures, and actual workflow adoption—not simply software procurement. [4]

## Decision Rule

The operating proposition becomes a live service only after a city has a named accountable owner, verified local partner chain, safety/handling procedure, consent and privacy controls, funded capacity, and an escalation route. Until then, Arctura’s honest product is **preparedness, coordination, and evidence**.
