# Arctura Family Foundation — Operating Charter

**Working Draft — legal, tax, insurance, food-safety, and payments review is required before public launch.**

## Mandate

Arctura Family Foundation exists to convert excess food, local supply, and philanthropic capital into reliable, dignified access to nourishment. It is not an ungoverned delivery marketplace or a generic donation funnel. Its operating standard is: **clear promises, accountable partners, safe fulfillment, and auditable impact**.

## Three-Layer Model

| Layer | Purpose | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Governs eligible charitable support, funds food-access programs, and measures impact | Never mixes charitable funds with commercial receipts or makes unsupported tax claims |
| Operating Platform | Manages partner intake, recipient requests, fulfillment coordination, service cases, and audit trails | Never presents illustrative inventory, routes, or service coverage as live |
| Commercial Services | Provides mission-aligned food-recovery coordination, reporting, training, and paid offerings | Never conditions recipient access on payment |

## Launch Geography

Los Angeles, Austin, and Denver are **research and partnership-development markets** until each has a named nonprofit recipient partner, food-safety procedure, insured carrier coverage, funded subsidy budget, and documented escalation owner.

## Non-Negotiable Controls

Every city cell must collect the minimum necessary data, preserve food traceability, retain a human escalation route, and publish only verified partner and service information. Gifts, sponsorships, grants, paid services, vendor obligations, and recipient subsidies must remain distinct in operations and accounting.

## Leadership Asset Rule

The supplied portraits and campaign images are treated as **private visual assets pending attribution approval**. They may only support a restrained leadership section after the person’s name, role, consent, and approved biography are supplied. They will not imply board membership, executive authority, donor status, partner endorsement, program impact, or affiliation.

## Launch Gates

| Gate | Evidence Required | Accountable Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entity and fundraising | IRS status or signed fiscal-sponsorship agreement; fundraising review | Governance lead |
| Food safety | Donation acceptance standards, temperature and traceability logs, recall process | Program operations lead |
| Fulfillment | Carrier terms, coverage verification, safe-drop and incident policies | Logistics lead |
| Finance | Accounting map, approval matrix, reconciliation process, payment activation | Finance lead |
| Privacy | Published notice, consent model, retention rules, access controls | Data steward |
| City activation | Verified partners, supply, service capacity, delivery budget, owner-on-call | City lead |
