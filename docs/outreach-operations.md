# Arctura — Consent-Aware Stakeholder Outreach Operations

**Name status:** “Arctura Family Foundation” is a working name only. The legal entity is pending creation and final-name selection.

## Operating Standard

Arctura uses stakeholder interest and consent records to support **manual, accountable follow-up**. It does not automate solicitation, campaigns, eligibility decisions, donor acknowledgements, recipient communications, or public claims.

| Queue | Record origin | Lifecycle | Manual control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Partner intake | Public partnership form | `new → review → qualified / paused / declined` | Qualification evidence and named owner required |
| Funding inquiries | Public funding-interest form | `new → review → qualified / closed` | No payment, tax, or solicitation workflow is activated |
| Consent requests | Public updates opt-in | `pending → confirmed / unsubscribed` | No message may be sent solely because a record is present |
| Support cases | Public correspondence form | `new → in progress → resolved` | Human response and minimal-data review required |

## Measurement Without Surveillance

The internal console may review bounded queue counts, status distribution, and first-party source labels to identify process gaps. It does not add tracking pixels, enrich profiles, infer protected characteristics, purchase contact lists, or combine recipient and donor records.

## Required Gates Before Any Outreach Activation

Before any email, SMS, CRM sequence, fundraising campaign, or sales workflow may be activated, Arctura must have a confirmed legal structure or fiscal sponsor where applicable, a published privacy notice, a documented consent model, unsubscribe handling, retention rules, named owner, approval matrix, and a tested human escalation route. Until then, stakeholder records are intake artifacts—not a marketing audience.
