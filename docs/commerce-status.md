# Commerce Status

> **Formation-stage notice.** Arctura Family Foundation is a working name. This document does not represent a formed legal entity, a charitable solicitation, a tax-deductible gift program, or an active food-delivery service.

## Current Boundary

Commerce is an **optional future channel**, not a prerequisite for Arctura’s local food-access preparation. The current catalog is not presented as an active public offering. No checkout or payment operation is authorized by this project documentation.

Before any paid, clearly non-charitable offering is considered, the following gates must be satisfied.

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Legal and financial separation | Commercial activity is separated from charitable gifts, grants, sponsorships, referral eligibility, and food-access services. |
| Product truthfulness | A real offering has a named owner, scope, price, image rights, buyer terms, and public disclosures. |
| Catalog readiness | The offering is intentionally configured, reviewed, and published to its intended channel. |
| Checkout approval | A responsible human explicitly approves checkout and any payment provider after legal and operational review. |

Until those gates are complete, Arctura will prioritize governance, local partner qualification, food-safety and fulfillment readiness, verified referral navigation, evidence discipline, and human stewardship.
