# Arctura Family Foundation — Finance and Contribution Rails

**Research date:** 18 August 2026. This is operating analysis, not legal, tax, banking, or merchant-underwriting advice.

## Contribution Rail

Givebutter documents support for major cards, ACH, PayPal, Venmo, digital wallets, and donor-advised funds. It also distinguishes digitally processed contributions from manually recorded offline cash, check, in-kind, stock, and property gifts. That breadth is useful for an eligible nonprofit contribution program, but it does not replace entity validation, gift acceptance policy, accounting controls, or donor acknowledgement obligations. [1]

## Fiscal Sponsorship Gate

The National Council of Nonprofits describes fiscal sponsorship as an arrangement in which a nonprofit sponsor assumes fiduciary oversight and receives and administers charitable contributions. A legitimate arrangement requires sponsor discretion and control over contributions, along with a written agreement covering compliance, acknowledgement, fees, and recordkeeping. [2]

## Arctura’s Required Separation

| Flow | Permitted System | Public Label | Required Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charitable gift | Eligible entity or documented fiscal-sponsor contribution system | “Support food access” only after eligibility confirmation | Donor acknowledgement and restricted-fund coding |
| Paid product or service | Commercial checkout | “Purchase” or “service” | Merchant, tax, order, and fulfillment reconciliation |
| Corporate sponsorship | Contract plus accounting records | “Sponsor a program” only when benefits and restrictions are documented | Executed agreement and purpose mapping |
| Grant | Fund-accounting system | Not customer-facing | Restricted-fund and deliverable tracking |
| Recipient food access | Program and fulfillment system | “No-cost food access” in activated areas only | Subsidy allocation and delivery confirmation |
