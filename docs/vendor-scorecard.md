# Arctura Family Foundation — Provisional Vendor Scorecard

**Purpose.** “Arctura Family Foundation” is a working name only; the legal entity is pending creation and final-name selection. This scorecard narrows the research field for controlled pilots. It is **not** a procurement decision, a recommendation to contract, or a representation that any provider is connected to Arctura. Every candidate requires local legal, safeguarding, insurance, data-processing, pricing, and operational review before approval.

## Evaluation method

Each candidate is rated from **1 (weak fit)** to **5 (strong fit)** against six criteria: mission fit, pilot readiness, operational control, data portability, cost transparency, and ability to scale. The weighted score is a directional screening tool; it does not replace a request for proposal, security review, or reference checks.

## Staged recommendation

| Stage | Proposed operating stance | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formation | Arctura-owned intake database, human partner review, and explicit finance boundaries | Prevents premature automation and unsupported service claims. |
| Local pilot | HubSpot for relationship management; a qualified local food-recovery partner; one competitively reviewed delivery provider; fund accounting appropriate to the legal structure | Reduces integration surface while establishing evidence. |
| Multi-city | Formal delivery RFP, mature CRM/marketing operations, dedicated support quality controls, and a finance system with restricted-fund reporting | Adds resilience only after the local model is proven. |
| International | Country-by-country legal, safeguarding, data-residency, fiscal, language, and partner diligence | Global scope must not override local accountability. |

## Procurement gates

No vendor progresses beyond discovery without a written scope, service-level expectations, data-processing review, pricing comparison, incident escalation path, termination and export rights, named operational owner, and measurable pilot success criteria.
