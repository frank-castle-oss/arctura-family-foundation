# Arctura — Finance and Commercial Boundaries

**Name status:** “Arctura Family Foundation” is a working name only. The legal entity is pending creation and final-name selection.

## Purpose

The operating platform records financial context without accepting funds, issuing charges, moving money, calculating tax treatment, or authorizing vendor payment. Every record begins as an internal control record, not a transaction.

| Workflow | Dedicated queue | Permitted purpose | Prohibited action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible gifts | Finance ledger — gift | Track a prospective or authorized charitable record after legal review | Solicitation, tax receipt, payment capture, or donor-account storage |
| Grants | Finance ledger — grant | Track grant proposals, restrictions, and controlled settlement evidence | Treating a proposal as received funding |
| Sponsorships | Sponsorship commitments | Track a corporate commitment, restriction, and contracting state | Recording sponsorship through the generic finance form |
| Commercial services | Commercial contracts and commercial invoices | Track paid mission-aligned work separately from recipient access | Conditioning food access on payment or calling commercial revenue charitable support |
| Vendor disbursements | Finance ledger — vendor disbursement | Record proposed/approved/issued/settled payables for review | Initiating a bank transfer, payout, or card payment |

## Control Rules

The generic finance procedure accepts **gifts, grants, invoices, and vendor disbursements** only. New sponsorship records are rejected there and must use the sponsorship-commitment workflow. Paid commercial work must be represented by a commercial contract before any related invoice record is tracked. No workflow activates a payment rail, creates tax language, or publishes financial data.

## Lifecycle Discipline

Finance records use `proposed → approved → issued → settled`, with `void` available for records that must not proceed. These states are administrative tracking states; they are not evidence of bank settlement, charitable eligibility, audit approval, or public impact.
