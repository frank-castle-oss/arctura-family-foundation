# Arctura Family Foundation — Ecosystem Research

**Research date:** 18 August 2026. This is a vendor-diligence shortlist, not an endorsement, service-availability confirmation, procurement award, or operational guarantee.

## Food-Recovery Coordination

FoodRecovery.org publicly describes an operating model that separates smaller donors and recipients from large food-industry programs and warehouse-capable community partners. This is a useful design lesson for Arctura: donor onboarding, recipient capacity, and large-volume logistics must be separate workflows with distinct qualification rules—not one generic marketplace. [1]

## Last-Mile Fulfillment

DoorDash’s Drive API documentation states that a delivery quote can be used to validate serviceability and delivery cost before fulfillment. This makes a carrier API suitable only after Arctura knows that an actual pickup site, recipient address policy, service area, food-safety procedure, and funding source are in place. Carrier integration must quote before it commits and retain the external delivery identifier for audit and exception handling. [2]

## Vendor Selection Rule

Every operational vendor must pass mission alignment, local coverage, data-processing terms, safeguarding and insurance review, service-level expectations, incident response, price transparency, pilot measurement, and termination/portability review. A market scan alone never constitutes approval.

## CRM and Marketing Operations

HubSpot for Nonprofits publicly presents relationship tracking, follow-up automation, forms, landing pages, search-oriented web publishing, and campaign tools. Its published nonprofit offer has jurisdictional, eligibility, new-customer, and annual-term conditions. It is therefore a plausible pilot-stage CRM candidate only after Arctura has an eligible entity and a clear consent model. [3]

Google for Nonprofits requires a charitable organization in good standing and country-specific eligibility verification. Its available offerings include Google Workspace for Nonprofits, Google Ad Grants, the YouTube Nonprofit Program, and Google Earth and Maps. These are post-verification channels; they are not assumptions for a pre-entity launch. [4]

## Shortlist by Operating Domain

| Domain | Candidate Category | Diligence Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food recovery | FoodRecovery.org, city food-recovery organizations, food-bank partners | Can they accept the food categories, geography, timing, and data-sharing model required? |
| Last mile | DoorDash Drive, Uber Direct, insured local carrier partner | What exact service areas, quote terms, insurance, and proof-of-delivery controls apply? |
| Support | Founder-led help desk followed by a multilingual managed-service provider | Can the provider meet language, safeguarding, data-processing, and escalation requirements? |
| CRM | HubSpot-style pilot CRM; Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud at scale | Which system is accountable for consent, relationship history, and program records? |
| Finance | Nonprofit fund-accounting platform | Can it segregate restricted funds, program costs, approvals, and vendor disbursements? |
